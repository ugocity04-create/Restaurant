Our Story

"La Maison Dorée was born from a simple dream - to create a place where food is not just eaten but experienced. Founded in 2010 by Chef Antoine Dubois, our restaurant has been serving exquisite French inspired cuisine in the heart of the city for over a decade. We believe that great food brings people together and every dish we serve is crafted with passion, precision and the finest locally sourced ingredients. Whether you are joining us for a romantic dinner, a family celebration or business lunch, we promise you an unforgettable experience that will keep you coming back".
